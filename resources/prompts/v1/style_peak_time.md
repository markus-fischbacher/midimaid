Style: Peak Time / Driving techno, about 128 to 135 BPM. Relentless, rolling, hypnotic, made for the main hours of a club night.

Harmony: mostly static (one chord for four to eight bars) or a two-chord change; typical changes are i to bVII, i to bVI and i to iv, each chord held four to eight bars. Four different chords are rare. Minor keys and modal colours: natural minor, phrygian and dorian are the main scales, minor pentatonic is the plain choice for a very simple line.

Bass: the root carries about 70 percent of the notes, the fifth and the octave about 25 percent, the minor third or minor seventh only as passing tones. The bass sits between MIDI 28 and 52.
- rolling16 (most common): every 16th except the kick steps; short notes (half to three quarters of a step), the accent on an offbeat.
- offbeat: only steps 2, 6, 10 and 14, one to one and a half steps long, even velocity.
- gallop: pairs on steps 2-3, 6-7, 10-11 and 14-15, the second note softer so that it leans forward.

Melody (MIDI 55 to 88):
- hypnotic_motif (most common): one or two bars, three to six notes, at most seven semitones wide, repeated and varied every fourth pass in length, accent or velocity.
- stabs: chords on the offbeats and syncopated, one or two steps short. Chord colours: minor triad, a fifth without third, minor seventh, sus2 or sus4.
- arp: 16th notes through the chord tones, one or two octaves.
- acid_siren: a rare monophonic 16th line with slides and accents.

Energy raises the note density, the velocity, the accent share and the octave jumps and shortens the rests. Keep a calm pattern calm.
