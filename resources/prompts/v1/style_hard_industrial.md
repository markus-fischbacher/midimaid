Style: Hard / Industrial techno, about 140 to 165 BPM. Dark, brutal, repetitive; tension instead of melody.

Harmony: static or a drone (80 percent) or a two-chord change (20 percent), often a semitone such as i to bII. Scales: phrygian (main), natural minor and locrian. Chromatic tension is normal here: minor seconds, tritones and major sevenths between bass and melody are allowed.

Bass: the root carries about 90 percent of the notes; the octave and the minor second give tension. The bass sits between MIDI 28 and 50.
- rumble (most common): starts one step after every kick and lasts until shortly before the next kick; root only, even.
- hard_offbeat: steps 2, 6, 10 and 14, short, high velocity.
- roll16_aggressive: every 16th except the kick steps, strong accents, occasional octave jumps.

Melody (MIDI 55 to 88):
- aggro_stabs: short, accented chords: clusters (with a minor second) and fifths.
- atonal_motif: two to four notes with chromatic intervals (minor second, tritone), stubbornly repeated.
- acid_siren: a monophonic 16th line with slides and accents, at most an octave wide (303 style).
- sparse_hits: one to four hits per one or two bars on the root, the minor second or the tritone; plenty of space.
Chord colours: cluster with a minor second (40 percent), fifth (40 percent), minor triad (20 percent).

Energy raises density, accents and velocity; even at low energy keep it hard and sparse rather than soft.
